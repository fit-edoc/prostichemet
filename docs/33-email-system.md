# Email System

## Sending Emails
- **Provider**: Initially integrate with Resend for transactional and campaign emails.
- **Abstraction**: Build an interface EmailProvider so we can later support direct SMTP connections (allowing users to plug in their own Google/Microsoft accounts).
- **Limits**: Respect user-defined and system daily sending limits to protect domain reputation.

## Email Generation
- Emails are generated by the AI based on the user's templates and the specific research evidence gathered for the lead.
- The user **must** have the ability to review and approve AI-generated emails before they are sent.

## Tracking & Webhooks
- Enable open and click tracking.
- Expose a webhook endpoint (/api/webhooks/email) to receive delivery statuses, bounces, and complaints from the provider.
- Update the emails and leads database tables accordingly upon receiving webhook events.

## Reply Handling
- Set up a sub-domain or reply-to address to catch replies.
- Route replies to the eply-classifier-worker.
- The worker uses Gemini to extract sentiment and intent, updating the CRM state (e.g., moving lead to "Positive Reply" or "Unsubscribed").
