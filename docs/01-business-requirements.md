# Business Requirements

## Business Problem

Small businesses often need outbound sales but lack:

- Market research capability
- Sales researchers
- Lead databases
- Lead qualification systems
- Personalized outreach systems
- CRM infrastructure
- Sales analytics

The product combines these workflows.

---

## Business Objectives

### Objective 1

Reduce time required to identify qualified prospects.

### Objective 2

Improve prospect quality through evidence-backed research.

### Objective 3

Reduce time required to create personalized outreach.

### Objective 4

Provide a single workflow from prospect discovery to CRM.

### Objective 5

Create measurable feedback between outreach results and future research.

---

## Business Metrics

### Acquisition

- Signups
- Activated workspaces
- Research projects created

### Activation

- ICP generated
- Research completed
- Leads discovered
- Campaign created

### Engagement

- Researches per workspace
- Leads reviewed
- Campaigns created
- Emails sent

### Sales

- Positive replies
- Meetings
- Opportunities
- Customers

### Revenue

- MRR
- ARPU
- Customer acquisition cost
- Retention
- Expansion revenue

---

## North Star Metric

Qualified opportunities generated per active workspace.

Do not use emails sent as the primary north-star metric.
