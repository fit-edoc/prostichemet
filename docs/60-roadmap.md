# Roadmap

## MVP (Months 1-3)
- Focus on end-to-end core workflow: Profile -> ICP -> Research -> Leads -> Basic Campaign.
- Single email provider integration (Resend).
- Basic AI classification of positive/negative replies.
- Manual CSV exports of leads.

## V1 (Months 4-6)
- Connect external inboxes via OAuth (Google, Microsoft).
- Advanced A/B testing for email campaigns.
- Better visual analytics and revenue attribution.
- Multi-step email sequences with delays and condition branches.
- Saved Research workflows and bulk actions.

## V2 (Months 7-12)
- Autonomous AI SDR mode (Agentic workflows where AI handles objections).
- Team collaboration features (multi-user workspaces, permissions).
- Integrations with external CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce).
- Support for multiple outbound channels (LinkedIn, Twitter).
